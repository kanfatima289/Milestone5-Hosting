Hidden / external chargesRECURRING CONFUSION

Users can read brokerage, service charges and an MTF haircut as unexplained deductions when the breakdown is hard to find.

✓
Separate platform fees from statutory leviesExchange/clearing fees, stamp duty, GST and DP charges appear in the breakdown.
✓
Point to the contract noteProduct, segment and order type affect charges; contract notes show line items.
✓
Clarify MTF haircutA risk-based reduction in usable collateral margin, not a separate cash fee.
